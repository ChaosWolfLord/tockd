Changelog

What changed in each version of Tockd, newest first.

  1. 0.8124 Oct 2026

    One phone can now be supervised and also supervise other phones, with a button to switch between the two views. Turn it on in Settings.

  2. 0.8024 Oct 2026

    Kids see chores and adults see tasks everywhere, and a small good-job message slides in at the top when the last required chore is done.

  3. 0.7924 Oct 2026

    The Screen time page is clearer: it shows the minutes you have left, and three cards explain how to earn more time, which apps are locked and when.

  4. 0.7823 Oct 2026

    The navigation bar icons now animate when you pick a tab: the clock's hands run forward, the house hops, the checklist pops and the dumbbell wiggles.

  5. 0.7723 Oct 2026

    A Settings button now sits at the top left of every main page.

  6. 0.7623 Oct 2026

    A Settings button on Home, the block page now names the chores that are left, the blocker notice updates straight away, the code is called the supervisor's code everywhere, and Settings is shorter.

  7. 0.7523 Oct 2026

    Clearer outlined buttons on the block page and Home, and the in-app privacy policy now links to the website version.

  8. 0.7422 Oct 2026

    Setup is shorter: chores are now one switch, with the chore list in Settings and the Tasks tab. Fixes a tap right after a back swipe opening the wrong page, and Back on the first setup step.

  9. 0.7322 Oct 2026

    Coins and the prize shop are gone: workouts simply add more unlock minutes. A brighter, more colourful look, a bigger Kid or Adult choice in setup, and friendlier locked and code screens.

  10. 0.7222 Oct 2026

    Setup now needs you to pick Kid or Adult before going on. Kids get a friendlier, more playful setup with simple words, emoji and bigger buttons.

  11. 0.7122 Oct 2026

    A shorter setup: five steps instead of seven, with fewer questions. The option for a supervisor setting up someone else's phone is gone, and spot checks now start off (turn them on in Protection).

  12. 0.7022 Oct 2026

    Setup now asks whether the phone is for a kid or an adult. For adults, school and homework become work hours and family time.

  13. 0.6922 Oct 2026

    A redesigned setup. One guided flow in seven steps: the idea, the supervisor's code, which apps to lock, when they're always locked, chores and spot checks, walking and workouts, and turning on the blocker. Related choices are together now, and the blocker and code are part of setup.

  14. 0.6821 Oct 2026

    In setup the last button now says Next, and pressing Back on the supervisor's page returns you to setup.

  15. 0.6721 Oct 2026

    Spot checks is now called Parent Phone Links in Protection. Workouts no longer ask for an age: everyone gets one standard plan.

  16. 0.6620 Oct 2026

    If a linked supervisor's phone goes quiet for days (for example Tockd was uninstalled there), Tockd says so and offers to unlink it. Linking the supervisor's phone again replaces the dead link.

  17. 0.6520 Oct 2026

    Fixes Tockd being impossible to uninstall: Android's app info and uninstall screens are no longer blocked.

  18. 0.6420 Oct 2026

    Behind-the-scenes update for the Google Play version. Nothing changes for you.

  19. 0.6320 Oct 2026

    Apps you install later are no longer blocked automatically; add them to the blocked list yourself.

  20. 0.6219 Oct 2026

    Bedtime and the other schedules only lock your blocked apps; the old Block everything wording is gone.

  21. 0.6119 Oct 2026

    On a supervisor's phone, Finished today lists each chore once.

  22. 0.6019 Oct 2026

    While Tockd is active, a quiet notification shows what's locked. Tockd is now described as a family app. Screen time counts every blocked app correctly.

  23. 0.5919 Oct 2026

    Much smaller app: about 5 MB instead of 55 MB, so updates download faster and use less space.

  24. 0.5819 Oct 2026

    Frees up space: downloaded updates are now deleted once installed (they were piling up in Tockd's cache).

  25. 0.5719 Oct 2026

    A linked supervisor's phone now gets a notification when Tockd's blocker is switched off (and when it's back on).

  26. 0.5619 Oct 2026

    The supervisor chooses spot checks right after setting the code. Lowering the daily step or calorie goal needs the code. A supervisor's phone now only notifies about spot checks.

  27. 0.5519 Oct 2026

    Closes loopholes: changing or deleting a required chore needs the code; apps installed later start out blocked; blocked apps in split screen or picture-in-picture are blocked too; rushed workouts don't count; steps typed in by hand don't count.

  28. 0.5419 Oct 2026

    Chores ticked off Wi-Fi count straight away, but one picked for a spot check waits for the supervisor's OK once the phone is back on Wi-Fi. Un-ticking a spot-checked chore and ticking it again no longer skips the check.

  29. 0.5319 Oct 2026

    Spot checks only happen while the phone is on Wi-Fi. Off Wi-Fi, ticked chores count straight away.

  30. 0.5218 Oct 2026

    Shows the new, shorter tour once, even if you've seen the old one.

  31. 0.5118 Oct 2026

    The tour (Settings > How Tockd works) is shorter: 3 pages, starting with your supervisor, who holds the code.

  32. 0.5018 Oct 2026

    Bedtime is one schedule every night now (no separate weekend bedtime), and setup lets you connect Health Connect for steps and calories.

  33. 0.4918 Oct 2026

    New setups no longer block social media and video apps automatically: the supervisor picks what to block.

  34. 0.4818 Oct 2026

    Setup: a name is required, 'Goes to school/work', and one bedtime for every night.

  35. 0.4718 Oct 2026

    Deleting your account now shows a confirmation screen before setup starts again.

  36. 0.4618 Oct 2026

    Blocked apps now shows only the apps on your phone. Sites like instagram.com stay blocked in the browser (see Websites at the bottom).

  37. 0.4518 Oct 2026

    Blocked apps now lists every app on your phone, with search, so any app can be blocked.

  38. 0.4418 Oct 2026

    New: a quick tour of every feature. It shows once, and it's always in Settings, How Tockd works.

  39. 0.4318 Oct 2026

    Uninstall protection has been removed. Updating turns it off automatically if it was on.

  40. 0.4218 Oct 2026

    Cleaner Workout tab: no more day row with A/B/C/R letters.

  41. 0.4118 Oct 2026

    Essential apps are gone: any app you don't block always works. School, homework, bedtime and Lock now keep the blocked apps locked.

  42. 0.4018 Oct 2026

    Tockd now says "supervisor" instead of "parent" for whoever holds the override code.

  43. 0.3918 Oct 2026

    Your streak now sets your rank: days in a row with the chores done. XP and levels are gone, and prizes no longer need a level.

  44. 0.3818 Oct 2026

    Prepared for Google Play. Small text fix on the lock screen.

  45. 0.3718 Oct 2026

    New Tockd icon: a clock inside the badge. Website moved to chaoswolflord.github.io/tockd.

  46. 0.3618 Oct 2026

    ScreenCheck is now called Tockd. Same app, same data; just a new name.

  47. 0.3518 Oct 2026

    Privacy policy (Settings, About). A clear explanation of what the blocker sees before it's turned on.

  48. 0.3417 Oct 2026

    Shorter Settings and Protection pages. Loosening a schedule now needs the parent's code (the 'wait 24 hours' option is gone).

  49. 0.3317 Oct 2026

    Shorter first-time setup: 3 quick pages instead of 6. Everything else starts with sensible settings you can change later.

  50. 0.3217 Oct 2026

    Spot checks: about 1 in 5 ticked chores waits for a parent's OK (turn on in Protection, Spot checks). A parent's own phone can run Tockd in parent mode and link to the child's phone over the home Wi-Fi to get finished chores and answer spot checks.

  51. 0.3116 Oct 2026

    Settings: Delete account (starts over at setup). Protection: turn off uninstall protection. Both need the parent's code.

  52. 0.3016 Oct 2026

    Version 1: set up for any family. Pick chores, prizes, bedtimes, school days and earning rules in setup, and turn workouts on or off.

Versions 0.8 to 0.29 were early test builds made for one family before Tockd was opened up to everyone, so they aren't listed.
