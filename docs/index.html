Tockd

Screen time is earned. Blocked apps unlock once the day's chores (and, if you like, a short workout) are done.

How it works

  • A supervisor (a parent, a partner, a friend, anyone you trust) picks which apps and websites are blocked and sets a private override code.
  • Chores, steps and workouts earn unlock minutes and coins. Days in a row with the chores done build a streak, and the streak sets your rank.
  • School hours, homework time and bedtime lock apps on a schedule.
  • Optional spot checks: about 1 in 5 ticked chores waits for the supervisor's OK, on the same phone or on the supervisor's own linked phone.
  • Loosening any rule needs the supervisor's code.

Your information stays with you

No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no Tockd servers. Everything stays on your own devices. Read the privacy policy.
