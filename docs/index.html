How it works

🔒

Apps get locked

A supervisor (a parent or guardian) picks the apps to lock, like games and videos. Everything else always works.

✅

Chores unlock them

Finish the day's chores and the apps open. Walking and workouts earn extra minutes.

🔑

A secret code keeps it fair

Only the supervisor knows the code. Changing a rule or unlocking early needs it, and every use is logged.

For kids

  • 🔥 Build a streak. Every day with the chores done adds one, and your streak sets your rank, from Iron up to Celestial.
  • 💪 Workouts add minutes. Short home workouts that get a little harder each time, for extra unlock time.
  • 🚶 Walking counts. Steps from Health Connect earn minutes too.
  • 🏅 Collect badges. Streaks and milestones get a celebration.

For parents

  • ⏰ Schedules. School hours, homework time and bedtime keep the chosen apps locked, even with minutes earned.
  • 🧐 Spot checks (optional). About 1 in 5 ticked chores waits for your OK. Chores ticked off Wi-Fi count for now and are checked once the phone is back on Wi-Fi.
  • 📱 Your own phone. Link it over the home Wi-Fi to see finished chores, answer spot checks, and be told if the blocker is switched off. Turn on "Also supervise other phones" to use Tockd yourself and switch between your phone and the phones you supervise.
  • 🔐 Loosening any rule needs your code. Tockd always logs when the code is used.

Set up in five steps

  1. Say whether the phone is for a kid or an adult, and add a name.
  2. The supervisor picks a secret code.
  3. Tick the apps that should be locked.
  4. Check the daily routine: school or work hours, homework or family time, bedtime, and whether chores unlock the apps.
  5. Turn on the blocker. Tockd explains what it does first.

🛡️ Your information stays with you

No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no Tockd servers. Everything stays on your own devices. The blocker only sees which app is open, never your messages or passwords. Read the privacy policy.
